THE BUILT WHERE

Why the world's big things stand exactly where they do.

The Built Where is a video channel about walls, dams, canals, cities and bridges, and what the ground, the water and the weather had to say about where they were built. Every episode is animated in 3D from real data: elevation models, satellite images, rain records and engineering reports. Every number on screen has a source, listed under the video.
