Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers this website and TBW Publisher, the publishing tool of The Built Where channel. TBW Publisher is used only by the channel's owner, to publish the channel's own videos on the channel's own accounts.

Visitors to this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or forms, and does not collect personal data. The host, GitHub Pages, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Data TBW Publisher uses

  • Google / YouTube: with the owner's permission, TBW Publisher uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and thumbnails to the channel and to read their processing and privacy status. It reads the channel's own basic details (name, handle, ID) to confirm the right channel.
  • TikTok, Instagram and Facebook: with the owner's permission, TBW Publisher uses their official APIs to upload the channel's videos and captions to its own accounts and to read their status.

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google account's third-party access settings, in TikTok's app permissions, and in Facebook and Instagram's business integrations. Stored tokens are then deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy: write through the contact details on the channel's About page.
